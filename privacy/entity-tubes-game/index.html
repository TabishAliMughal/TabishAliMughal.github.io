Entity Tubes Game — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 17 November 2026
App: Entity Tubes Game (Android package com.entity.tubes.game)
Developer: Tabish Ali Mughal

Entity Tubes Game (“the app”) is a casual puzzle game made for children and families by Tabish Ali Mughal (“we”, “us”). This policy explains what information the app does and does not handle. In short: we do not collect any personal information.

1. No accounts, no personal data collected by us

The app has no sign-up, login or account of any kind. We do not ask for, collect, store or share names, email addresses, phone numbers, photos, contacts, location, or any other personal information. We do not run our own servers that receive data from the app.

2. Game progress stays on your device

Your progress, settings and scores are saved only in the app's local storage on your device. They are never uploaded to us. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data deletes them permanently.

3. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app is free and shows occasional full-screen ads provided by Google AdMob, a service of Google LLC. Because the app is designed for children, every ad request is configured as follows:

  • Child-directed: every request is tagged for child-directed treatment (tagForChildDirectedTreatment) and as a user under the age of consent (tagForUnderAgeOfConsent).
  • Family-safe content only: the maximum ad content rating is set to G (general audiences).
  • Non-personalised ads only: no interest-based advertising, no remarketing, no behavioural profiling.
  • No advertising ID: the app does not collect the Android Advertising ID; the AD_ID permission is removed from the app.

To deliver an ad and protect against fraud, Google AdMob may still process limited technical information, such as the device's IP address (to deliver the ad, and for approximate, country-level location), and basic device and app information (device model, operating system version, app version, language) for ad delivery, measurement and fraud/abuse prevention. This processing is done by Google under its own policies, with the child-directed restrictions above applied. See Google's Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

4. No analytics or tracking

The app contains no third-party analytics, crash-reporting, attribution or tracking SDKs. Google AdMob (described above) is the only third-party service in the app.

5. Update check

When the app starts with an internet connection, it downloads a small public file from our website (https://tabishalimughal.github.io/manifest.json) that lists the latest app version, so it can tell you if an update is required. This is a plain download: the app sends no personal information, identifiers or game data with it. Like any web request, the hosting provider (GitHub Pages) receives the device's IP address to deliver the file; we do not receive or store it. If you are offline, the app works normally.

6. Permissions

The app uses internet access only to load ads and perform the update check described above. It does not request access to your camera, microphone, contacts, location, photos or files.

7. Children's privacy (COPPA and GDPR-K)

The app is intended for children and participates in the Google Play Families program. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13 (US COPPA) or under the applicable age of digital consent in the EU/UK (GDPR Article 8 / UK Age Appropriate Design Code). There is nothing to request, correct or delete because we hold no personal data. Ads are restricted as described in section 3, and we do not show a consent prompt because no personalised advertising or tracking takes place. Parents or guardians with any question may contact us using the details below.

8. Data sharing and selling

We do not sell, rent or share any personal information, because we do not collect any.

9. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new “Last updated” date.
