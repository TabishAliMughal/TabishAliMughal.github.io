Our approach

  • Kid-safe by design

    Built for Google Play's Families rules: made for kids, no accounts, and only the permissions a game needs.

  • Child-safe ads

    Ads are non-personalised, family-rated (G) and child-directed, with no advertising ID.

  • No tracking

    No analytics and no personal data collected. Each game's privacy policy spells it out.

  • Offline-first

    Every game plays without an internet connection, and progress is saved on the device.

  • 5 difficulties × 50 levels

    Very Easy, Easy, Medium, Hard and Expert, each numbered 1-50 and unlocked level by level.
