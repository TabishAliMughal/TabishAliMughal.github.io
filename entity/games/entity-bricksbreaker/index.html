About the game

A turn-based arcade puzzle. Rows of numbered neon bricks creep down one row each turn - aim, fire a stream of bouncing sparks, and break every brick before one reaches the red line.

Two ways to play

Levels

60 levels in 5 tiers, from Very Easy to Expert. Bricks move down one row after every volley, and each level earns 1 to 3 stars.

Infinity New

Pick Easy, Medium, Hard or Expert - nothing is locked. Bricks slide down without stopping while your sparks stream from the launcher on their own; you steer the aim. Clear every brick on the board to win the stage.

How to play

  1. Drag on the board, or use the angle slider, to aim. A dotted guide previews the shot and its first bounce.
  2. Release to fire a volley of sparks that bounce off walls and bricks.
  3. Each hit lowers a brick's number - at zero it bursts.
  4. Collect green +1 rings to add more sparks to your next volley.
  5. Clear every brick before any reaches the red line.

Infinity mode

  • Four difficulties: Easy, Medium, Hard and Expert, each with its own saved stage and records.
  • Win a stage by clearing every brick on the board. Faster clears earn more stars, and a time bonus adds to the stage score.
  • Each new stage is a little harder: bricks move a bit faster, carry bigger numbers, and every 4 stages each new row holds at least one more brick.
  • Your score carries over: each stage you clear adds to a running total. Lose, and that total is saved as your best score if it beats the old one, then you retry the same stage - you never drop back a stage.
  • Pause any time; the game also pauses itself when you leave the app.

Controls

  • Angle slider - slide left or right to aim, or nudge it one degree at a time with the buttons beside it. The dotted guide follows live. In levels, lifting your finger after a slide (or a short hold) fires the volley; a quick tap only moves the slider. In Infinity it steers the stream of sparks.
  • Recall - in levels, pulls the sparks still flying back to the launcher; sparks not yet fired stay in your pool. The turn then ends as usual and the bricks move down.
  • Fast - doubles the speed of a volley.

Features

  • 60 levels in 5 tiers, Very Easy to Expert, plus endless Infinity mode
  • 1 to 3 stars per level and per Infinity stage
  • Square and triangle bricks
  • Row Zap helper clears the lowest row - earn one every 5 levels (hold up to 3)
  • Light and dark themes, or follow your phone's setting - both tested for readable contrast
  • Gentle on-screen tutorial on level 1
  • Free retries with no lives system
