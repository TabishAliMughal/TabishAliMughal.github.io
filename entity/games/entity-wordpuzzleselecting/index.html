About the game

A crossword-style word puzzle: choose letters from a letter bank and place them in a numbered grid, guided by across and down clues. Words cross on shared letters just like a real crossword, across five difficulty tiers.

Screenshots

How to play

  1. Read the across and down clues.
  2. Tap a letter in the letter bank, then tap a grid cell to place it.
  3. A wrong letter costs one life; run out and the level simply restarts.
  4. Fill every word to complete the puzzle, with up to 3 free hints if needed.

Features

  • 250 levels: 5 tiers (Very Easy to Expert) of 50 levels each
  • Real crossword layout where words cross on shared letters
  • Lives scaled to each puzzle's size - no lockout, just retry
  • 3-star rating based on mistakes
  • Up to 3 hints per level that reveal a correct letter at no life cost
  • System, Light or Dark theme
