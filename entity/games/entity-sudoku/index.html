About the game

A Sudoku game with a daily puzzle shared by every player on the same date, plus Free Play at any of five difficulties. Build a daily streak, celebrate milestones and track your stats.

How to play

  1. Pick the Daily puzzle or a Free Play difficulty.
  2. Select a cell and tap a number to fill it in.
  3. Use pencil marks for candidates, and Undo or Erase to fix mistakes.
  4. Fill the grid so every row, column and 3x3 box has the digits 1 to 9.

Features

  • Five difficulties from Very Easy to Expert - 1,250 puzzles in total
  • Daily mode (the same puzzle for everyone each day) and Free Play
  • Streaks with milestone celebrations at 7, 30, 100 and 365 days, plus a 30-day calendar
  • Pencil marks, Undo, Erase and up to 3 hints per puzzle
  • Optional auto-check and highlighting of matching numbers, row, column and box
  • Progress saves automatically mid-game
  • Light, Dark or System theme
