About the game

A calm underwater bubble shooter. Aim and shoot glossy sea-bubbles into a cluster hanging from the reef - match 3 or more of the same colour to pop them, and anything left hanging floats away.

Screenshots

How to play

  1. Drag to aim; a dotted guide shows the shot, including wall bounces.
  2. Release to shoot. Tap the next bubble to swap it with the current one.
  3. Match 3 or more bubbles of the same colour to pop them.
  4. Bubbles that lose their link to the ceiling drop away for bonus points.
  5. Clear the cluster before it reaches the danger line - misses make it creep lower.

Features

  • 60 levels in 5 tiers, Very Easy to Expert, with 1 to 3 stars per level
  • Colour-blind friendly: every bubble colour carries its own shape glyph
  • Bomb bubble (earned when one shot drops 8+ bubbles) and a free, unlimited Swap
  • Guided onboarding on level 1
  • Respects reduced-motion settings (calmer effects, no screen shake)
  • Progress saved, including mid-level
