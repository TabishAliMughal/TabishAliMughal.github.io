Entity Tubes Game

Pour, sort and match colours tube by tube.

Coming soon to Google Play Privacy policy

About the game

A relaxing colour-sorting puzzle: pour colours between tubes until every tube holds a single colour. 50 levels across three difficulty tiers, with an animated pour and a 3-star celebration when you win.

Screenshots

How to play

  1. Tap a tube to select it.
  2. Tap another tube to pour the top colour across.
  3. You can only pour onto an empty tube or a matching top colour, and never into a full tube.
  4. Win when every tube holds just one colour, or is empty.

Features

  • 50 levels in 3 tiers: Easy, Medium and Hard
  • Unlimited undo, plus reset to restart the level
  • Level select showing completed and current levels
  • Animated pour stream and a 3-star win celebration
  • System, Light or Dark appearance
  • Progress saved on your device

Safe for families
