About the game

A hexagon word puzzle: drag across the lettered corners to spell words, and find enough of them to clear the level. Five difficulty tiers from Very Easy to Expert, with hints, scoring and progress tracking.

Screenshots

Swipe or scroll sideways; select a screenshot to open it full size.

How to play

  1. Each level shows a hexagon with 4 to 8 lettered corners.
  2. Drag your finger across the corners to spell a word, then lift to submit it.
  3. Find the target number of words to complete the level.
  4. Stuck? Up to 3 hints: a definition, then the first half of the word, then its length.

Features

  • 246 levels across 5 tiers, Very Easy to Expert
  • 3 hints per level that reveal a little more each time
  • Longer words score more, with a bonus for finishing faster
  • A shake and red flash when a word isn't valid, plus optional haptics
  • System, Light or Dark theme
  • Progress and best scores saved on your device
